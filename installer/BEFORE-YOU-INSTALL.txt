J.A.R.V.I.S. - before you install
=================================

What you need
-------------
  - Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
  - An internet connection during setup
  - A microphone and speakers (for talking to J.A.R.V.I.S.)
  - Free disk space: about 3 GB (Standard), about 9 GB (Full, with offline mode)

What the setup installs for you (if they are not there already)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Using Windows' own installer (winget). Windows may ask for permission once
for each of them.
  - Node.js           runs J.A.R.V.I.S.
  - Python 3.11       voice, documents and the free AI accounts
  - Google Chrome     the J.A.R.V.I.S. window
  - ffmpeg            video and picture editing
  - Ollama            offline answers (Full / offline mode only)

Then it downloads J.A.R.V.I.S.'s packages and, depending on what you choose,
the speech models. A window shows the progress; it takes about 15-30 minutes
(Standard) and longer for Full (about 5 GB more), depending on the internet.
One step - the Python packages - shows no progress for several minutes:
that is normal, please leave the window open.

Desktop switches (you can choose them during setup)
---------------------------------------------------
  - JARVIS - ON / OFF        the kill switch: double-click to turn J.A.R.V.I.S.
                             and "Hey Jarvis" off (or back on). Stays off
                             across restarts until switched on again.
  - JARVIS Popup - ON / OFF  the small popup as J.A.R.V.I.S.'s face, or the
                             main window.
Both are also in the Start menu.

After setup
-----------
  - J.A.R.V.I.S. opens and asks your name and how you want it to sound.
  - To think, it needs at least one AI account. The free ones (Gemini,
    OpenRouter, Groq) are set up from its Customize screen by signing in -
    nothing to copy or paste. A Claude subscription is optional.
  - Say "health check" at any time: it tells you what is missing and how to
    fix it.

Your data
---------
Your settings, keys, memory and reminders are kept on this PC only, in
%USERPROFILE%\.jarvis. Uninstalling asks whether to keep them.
